Our Story 📖

First Meeting

We first met when you came to see Moksha's fine arts work in Canvas Society during our first year. Little did I know how helpful you'd turn out to be.

Different Campuses, Same Respect

Even though we're from different campuses (me from East Campus, you from Main Campus) and didn't interact constantly, every single conversation has been respectful, kind, and positive.

The OA Incident

I was giving an OA at main campus and my laptop bugged out. I panicked and called several people who didn't pick up. You picked up immediately and rushed to the exam hall to give me your laptop. I didn't get shortlisted, but the gesture mattered so much that I had to thank you with a chocolate. 🍫

Always Reliable

Whenever I needed any small help from main campus (like ID card info) while sitting at East Campus, one call to you and the help was instant, with absolutely no hesitation.

Genuine Moral Support

When I left Canvas Society due to placement pressure, I was feeling incredibly low and even cried. I told you about it, expecting judgment, but instead, you listened. You told me not to be demotivated, that this was just a phase, and reminded me that the society was meant to be a stress relief. You said, "Ek din toh aaega hi". I admitted I was bad at expressing my feelings and really only talked to you in the society. You understood my nature completely. That genuine support is something I'll never forget. ❤️
